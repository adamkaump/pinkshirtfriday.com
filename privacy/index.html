Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

In short: my apps don't collect, store or share your personal data. Anything an app saves stays on your device, or in your own iCloud account if the app syncs.

This policy covers every app published by Adam Kaump under the Pink Shirt Friday name ("I", "me"). The sections for individual apps below describe anything specific to that app.

Data I collect

None. My apps have no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting services or tracking of any kind, and I don't operate servers that they send your data to.

Data stored on your device

Apps may save things like your progress and settings so they're there next time. This stays on your device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and is removed when you delete the app.

Some apps can sync that data between your devices using iCloud. It's stored in your own private iCloud account, which I can't see or access, and you can turn it off in your device's iCloud settings.

Services provided by Apple

Some apps use features provided by Apple, such as Apple Maps, iCloud and Game Center. Those features are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy, and I don't receive any information from them.

Children

Because my apps collect no data, they collect no data from children.

Pinpoint

  • Saved on your device: your quiz answers (which place was asked, what you tapped, whether you were right, how far off you were and how long you took) and your quiz settings, used to show your stats.
  • Location: Pinpoint does not ask for or use your location.
  • Apple Maps: the globe and satellite imagery come from Apple through MapKit. To draw the map, your device requests imagery from Apple for the area you're viewing.

Spider Solitaire

  • Saved on your device: a record of each finished game (date, difficulty, whether you won, score, moves and time), your settings, and the game in progress so you can pick it up later.
  • iCloud: your game history and settings sync between your devices through your private iCloud account. I can't see or access it.
  • Game Center: if you're signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are reported to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile. You can sign out in your device's settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date. If an app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.
