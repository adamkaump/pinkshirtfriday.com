Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

In short: my apps don't collect your personal data. They send me usage reports under a random ID that isn't tied to your name, account or device, and anything you choose to send through Send Feedback. Rerun also has an optional account, described below. Everything else an app saves stays on your device, in your own iCloud account, or with the people you choose to share it with.

This policy covers every app published by Adam Kaump under the Pink Shirt Friday name ("I", "me").

Data I collect

My apps have no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking of any kind, and apart from Rerun's optional account, no accounts. They send only the things below, to small servers I run on Cloudflare. None of it is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Usage reports

Each app makes a random ID when you first use it and replaces it with a new one every year. The ID is made on your device and isn't your name, account, Apple ID, advertising identifier or any ID from your device, and it's never shared with anyone. It lets me count how many different people use an app and for how long, without knowing who they are.

Once a day when you use an app, it sends a short report with that ID and:

  • The app, its version and build number.
  • Your device model (for example "iPhone 16"), operating system and version, and preferred language.

While an app is on screen, it also sends the ID, the app's version and whether you've just opened it about once a minute. The server records when each visit started and ended. This is how I count people, sessions and time spent in the app each day, and how many people are using it right now.

When an app shows you an announcement, it also reports that the announcement was seen and whether you tapped its link, without the ID.

Your IP address is used only to stop abuse and is never stored; the server keeps only the country it comes from. I only look at totals, never at one ID's activity. Usage data is deleted after 13 months.

Feedback you send

If you use Send Feedback in an app, I receive your message, the email address you give (if any), and the app version, device model, operating system version, language and country. I use them only to read and reply to your message. Your IP address isn't stored. Feedback is kept until I've dealt with it, and I'll delete yours on request.

Rerun accounts and sharing

Rerun plays movies and shows from a media server you run yourself. Your media, watch history, clips and searches go only between the app and your server, never through my servers.

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. Rerun doesn't ask Apple for your name or email address. My account service stores:

  • An account ID, and the ID Apple gives Rerun for you.
  • The media servers you've claimed: each server's name and software version, its addresses on your home network, and the public IP address it connects from, so the app can find it at home and away.
  • The libraries you've shared or joined. When you invite someone, the name you give them (for example "Sam") is stored with their invite and membership.
  • If your server uses Rerun's provided movie information or AI features, how much AI each account uses each day, to apply limits.

You can delete your account in Rerun's settings. That deletes the account and its memberships and invites, and unlinks your servers.

Anyone can try Rerun's demo library without signing in. Your progress in it is stored on my server under a random pass, with no name or account, and deleted 90 days after it last changes.

Rerun shows posters and artwork from The Movie Database (TMDB), loaded directly from TMDB's image servers.

Data stored on your device

Apps save things like your progress, game or quiz history, settings, grocery lists and recipes, so they're there next time. This is never sent to me, and is removed from a device when you delete the app there.

iCloud sync

Some apps sync that data between your devices using iCloud. It's stored in your own private iCloud account, which I can't see or access, and you can turn it off in your device's iCloud settings.

Sharing

When you share a Kitchen Sync household, the people you invite can see and edit its list, recipes and stores, and the name you give yourself. A shared household is stored in the iCloud account of the person who shared it. You can stop sharing, or leave a household you joined, at any time.

Location

Kitchen Sync can use your location, if you allow it, to switch to the store you're at and to remind you of your list when you arrive. Your location is checked on your device and never sent to me. Store addresses you save are shared with your household. My other apps don't ask for or use your location.

Services provided by Apple

Some apps use features provided by Apple. These are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy, and I don't receive any information from them.

  • Apple Maps: apps with maps request map imagery from Apple for the area you're viewing. When you look up a store in Kitchen Sync, your search is sent to Apple Maps.
  • Apple Intelligence and Image Playground: Kitchen Sync uses these to guess aisles, suggest ingredients and draw icons. They run on your device.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: if you use Siri with Kitchen Sync, your requests are handled by Apple.
  • Game Center: if you're signed in to Game Center, apps that support it report your achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple under your Game Center profile. You can sign out in your device's settings.
  • iCloud: used only for the sync and sharing described above.

Children

My apps don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage reports use a random ID rather than anything that identifies the person, and feedback includes only what the sender chooses to write.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in any app.
