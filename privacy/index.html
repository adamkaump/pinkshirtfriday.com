Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

In short: my apps don't collect your personal data. They send me anonymous counts of how many people use them, which can't be tied to you, and anything you choose to send through Send Feedback. Everything else an app saves stays on your device, in your own iCloud account, or with the people you choose to share it with.

This policy covers every app published by Adam Kaump under the Pink Shirt Friday name ("I", "me").

Data I collect

My apps have no accounts, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking of any kind. They send only the two things below, to a small server I run on Cloudflare. Neither is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Anonymous usage counts

Once a day when you use an app, it sends a short report so I can count how many people use it each day, week and month. The report contains:

  • The app, its version and build number.
  • Your device model (for example "iPhone 16"), operating system and version, and preferred language.
  • Whether it's the app's first report this week and this month. This is how weekly and monthly users are counted without identifying anyone.

When an app shows you an announcement, it also reports that the announcement was seen and whether you tapped its link.

These reports contain no name, account, advertising identifier or any other ID, so they can't be connected to you or to each other. Your IP address is used only to stop abuse and is never stored; the server keeps only the country it comes from. Reports are deleted after 13 months.

Feedback you send

If you use Send Feedback in an app, I receive your message, the email address you give (if any), and the app version, device model, operating system version, language and country. I use them only to read and reply to your message. Your IP address isn't stored. Feedback is kept until I've dealt with it, and I'll delete yours on request.

Data stored on your device

Apps save things like your progress, game or quiz history, settings, grocery lists and recipes, so they're there next time. This is never sent to me, and is removed from a device when you delete the app there.

iCloud sync

Some apps sync that data between your devices using iCloud. It's stored in your own private iCloud account, which I can't see or access, and you can turn it off in your device's iCloud settings.

Sharing

When you share a Kitchen Sync household, the people you invite can see and edit its list, recipes and stores, and the name you give yourself. A shared household is stored in the iCloud account of the person who shared it. You can stop sharing, or leave a household you joined, at any time.

Location

Kitchen Sync can use your location, if you allow it, to switch to the store you're at and to remind you of your list when you arrive. Your location is checked on your device and never sent to me. Store addresses you save are shared with your household. My other apps don't ask for or use your location.

Services provided by Apple

Some apps use features provided by Apple. These are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy, and I don't receive any information from them.

  • Apple Maps: apps with maps request map imagery from Apple for the area you're viewing. When you look up a store in Kitchen Sync, your search is sent to Apple Maps.
  • Apple Intelligence and Image Playground: Kitchen Sync uses these to guess aisles, suggest ingredients and draw icons. They run on your device.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: if you use Siri with Kitchen Sync, your requests are handled by Apple.
  • Game Center: if you're signed in to Game Center, apps that support it report your achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple under your Game Center profile. You can sign out in your device's settings.
  • iCloud: used only for the sync and sharing described above.

Children

My apps don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Usage counts are anonymous, and feedback includes only what the sender chooses to write.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in any app.
