Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

In short: my apps don't collect, store or share your personal data. Anything an app saves stays on your device, or in your own iCloud account if the app syncs.

This policy covers every app published by Adam Kaump under the Pink Shirt Friday name ("I", "me").

Data I collect

None. My apps have no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting services or tracking of any kind, and I don't operate servers that they send your data to.

Data stored on your device

Apps save things like your progress, game or quiz history, settings and any game in progress, so they're there next time. This stays on your device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and is removed when you delete the app.

iCloud sync

Some apps sync that data between your devices using iCloud. It's stored in your own private iCloud account, which I can't see or access, and you can turn it off in your device's iCloud settings.

Services provided by Apple

Some apps use features provided by Apple. These are covered by the Apple Privacy Policy, and I don't receive any information from them.

  • Apple Maps: apps with maps request map imagery from Apple for the area you're viewing.
  • Game Center: if you're signed in to Game Center, apps that support it report your achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple under your Game Center profile. You can sign out in your device's settings.
  • iCloud: used only for the sync described above.

Location

My apps don't ask for or use your location.

Children

Because my apps collect no data, they collect no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date. If an app ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact
