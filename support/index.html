Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? I'd love to hear it.

Email support@pinkshirtfriday.com. I read every message and usually reply within a few days. If you're reporting a problem, mention which app and device you're using.

Pinpoint

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Pinpoint has no accounts and doesn't sync, so stats on your iPhone and your Mac are kept separately. Deleting the app deletes your history.

Does Pinpoint work offline?

The quizzes and borders are built into the app, but the satellite imagery comes from Apple Maps and needs an internet connection to load areas you haven't viewed recently.

How is distance scoring calculated?

For cities, capitals and peaks, you earn more points the closer your tap is to the real spot. The distance that counts as "close" scales with the size of the map, so a world quiz is more forgiving than a quiz on a single country.

I can't find a small country or island.

When borders are on, places too small to tap easily are drawn with a circle around them. Tap anywhere inside the circle, or zoom in for a closer look.

How do I make a quiz harder?

Open the menu, choose a quiz, and turn off Show Borders. Borders are revealed after each answer so you can still learn from it.

A border or name looks wrong.

Borders come from Natural Earth, a public-domain map dataset, and some are disputed or simplified. If something looks off, please email me with the place and quiz.
