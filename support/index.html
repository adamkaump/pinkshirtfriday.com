Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? I'd love to hear it.

Email support@pinkshirtfriday.com. I read every message and usually reply within a few days. If you're reporting a problem, mention which app and device you're using.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, and in your private iCloud account so it follows you to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If iCloud is off for an app, its progress stays on that device only. Deleting an app deletes the progress saved on that device.

Do the apps work offline?

Yes, with one exception: map imagery comes from Apple Maps and needs an internet connection to load areas you haven't viewed recently. Syncing catches up the next time you're online.

My achievements or scores aren't showing up.

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in your device's settings. Achievements are worked out from your whole history, so anything you've already earned is credited the next time you finish a game.

How do I learn how to play?

Each app has help built in. In Spider Solitaire, open Settings for How to Play and Tips & Tricks.

Something looks wrong.

Please email me with the app, your device, and what you saw. A screenshot helps a lot. Spider Solitaire can also copy the current board from Settings → Copy Board State to paste into your email.
