One list for the whole household.

Kitchen Sync is a grocery list you share with everyone you live with. Add something on your phone and it's on everyone's list a moment later, sorted by aisle in the order you walk your store.

Coming soon to theApp Store

Shopping, sorted

Everything you need for a grocery list you share, and nothing you don't.

Share with your household

Invite the people you live with from Settings. Everyone sees the same list, and changes reach every device within moments.

Sorted by aisle

Items land in the aisle where you'll find them. Rename, add and reorder aisles to match the way you walk your store.

Type anything

Pick from common groceries or type your own. Kitchen Sync guesses the aisle and an emoji, and remembers when you change them. You can even make an icon with Image Playground.

Recipes

Save the meals you make. Search for one while adding items to see every ingredient, then add what's missing with a tap. Apple Intelligence can suggest the ingredients for you.

Checked off, not forgotten

Tap an item once it's in the cart. It leaves the list but stays in search with its aisle and icon, ready for next time.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your list lives in iCloud and is shared only with the people you invite. Available in nine languages.
