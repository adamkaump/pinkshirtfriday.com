One list for the whole household.

Kitchen Sync is a grocery list you share with everyone you live with. Add something on your phone and it's on everyone's list a moment later, sorted by aisle in the order you walk whichever store you're in.

Shopping, sorted

Everything you need for a grocery list you share, and nothing you don't.

Share with your household

Send an invite by Messages or a link. Everyone sees the same list, and changes show up on every device within moments.

Every store, its own order

Add the stores you shop at, each with its aisles in the order you walk them. Kitchen Sync switches to the store you walk into, and can remind you of your list when you arrive.

Just ask Siri

Say “Add groceries in Kitchen Sync” and list what you need. Siri can also read your list by aisle, check things off and add a recipe's ingredients.

Varieties and notes

Jot a brand or variety under any item. Need two kinds of bread? Add both, each with its own note.

Checked off, not forgotten

Tap an item once it's in the cart. It leaves the list but stays in search with its aisle and icon, ready for next time.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your list lives in iCloud and is shared only with the people you invite.

Close enough is good enough

Type “sause” and Kitchen Sync still finds Pasta sauce and Soy sauce. A missed letter or a quick misspelling never stands between you and what you need.

It knows where things go

Add something new, like Sriracha or Kombucha, and Apple Intelligence picks its aisle and an emoji, right on your device. Change either one and Kitchen Sync remembers for next time.

Icons of your own

Give any item a picture of its own with Image Playground, drawn to sit right alongside the emoji.

Shop for a whole recipe

Search for Tacos while adding items and every ingredient appears. Add the ones you're out of, or all of them at once.

Ingredients, suggested

Name a recipe and Apple Intelligence suggests what to buy for it, matched to the items your household already uses. Tap the ones you want.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro

One app, built natively for every Apple device you have, in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese. Your list follows you to all of them through iCloud.
