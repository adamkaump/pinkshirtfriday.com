Privacy Policy

Pinpoint · Effective October 23, 2026

In short: Pinpoint doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. Your quiz history stays on your device.

This policy covers the Pinpoint app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro, developed by Adam Kaump ("I", "me").

Data I collect

None. Pinpoint has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting services or tracking of any kind, and I don't operate any server that the app talks to.

Data stored on your device

To show your stats, Pinpoint saves your quiz answers (which place was asked, what you tapped, whether you were right, how far off you were and how long you took) and your quiz settings. This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to me or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

Pinpoint does not ask for or use your location.

Apple Maps

The globe and satellite imagery are provided by Apple through MapKit. To draw the map, your device requests map imagery from Apple for the area you're viewing. Those requests are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; I don't receive them.

Children

Pinpoint is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date. If Pinpoint ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@pinkshirtfriday.com.
