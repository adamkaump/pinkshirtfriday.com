King to Ace, eight times over.

Spider Solitaire is the classic two-deck patience game. Build runs in a single suit, clear all eight, and take your time: undo goes back as far as you like.

The game you know, done right

Pick one, two or four suits, from a relaxed game to a fiendish one, and start dealing.

Tap or drag

Drag a card where you want it, or just tap it and it finds the best spot on its own. Legal drop spots light up as you go.

Unlimited undo

Back out of any move, as far back as you like. Each undo costs a point, so your best scores still mean something.

Knows when you're stuck

If there's nothing useful left to do, Spider Solitaire tells you, so you're never left hunting for a move that isn't there.

Scores and Game Center

Score, moves and time for every game, plus your win rate. Achievements and leaderboards for best scores and fastest wins at every difficulty.

Play your way

Built-in How to Play and Tips & Tricks guides for when you're learning, and a left-handed mode that puts the controls on your side.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your games sync through your own iCloud account.

Move whole runs

Pick up a run of one suit and carry it in one go. The spot it can land on lights up green, or just tap the run and it finds the best place itself.

King to Ace, and it's gone

Build a full suit from King down to Ace and it clears off the board on its own. Two down, six to go.

Deal when you're ready

Out of moves? Deal a fresh card onto every column. The deck shows how many deals you have left, and if you're truly stuck, Spider Solitaire tells you.

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro

One app, built natively for every Apple device you have.
